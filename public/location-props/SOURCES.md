Original generic sand footprint field-effect sprites from Pokémon FireRed/LeafGreen, via pret/pokefirered: https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/tree/master/graphics/field_effects/pics
The UI shows the first 16×16 frame, tinted to match the ground. These are decorative tracks and are not culprit-specific evidence.

Paw and claw artwork: original Growlithe and Sandshrew Pokédex footprint sprites, respectively, from https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/tree/master/graphics/pokemon . Footprint selection depends only on the scene label (paw prints for dirt, claws for gravel), never on the culprit. Rendered with multiply blending to preserve pixel edges and remove the white sprite background.
